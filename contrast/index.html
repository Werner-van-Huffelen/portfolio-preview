About

Build something real, put it in people’s hands, and let them show you what to build next.

I love the moment a rough idea becomes something people actually use. To get there, I start with them: their work, their workarounds, the reasons nobody wrote down. With AI in the loop I design straight into the code, so they can try something real within days. I’m Dutch, so I’ll tell you what I think. Nicely.
